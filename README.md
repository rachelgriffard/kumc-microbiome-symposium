# The Microbial City - An Introduction to Microbiome Analysis

## KUMC Microbiome Symposium 2026

Companion site for an invited workshop delivered at the 
[2nd Annual Microbiome Symposium](https://events.kucancercenter.org/2nd-annual-microbiome-symposium) 
at the University of Kansas Medical Center (June 25, 2026), hosted by the 
KU Cancer Center and attended by researchers across cancer, immunology, 
microbiology, gastroenterology, and related fields.

## About the Workshop

This workshop was designed for wet lab biologists and clinician-researchers 
with little to no prior bioinformatics experience. The goal was to demystify 
the computational analysis of 16S rRNA microbiome sequencing data, covering 
not just the *how*, but the *why* behind each analytical decision. The goal
is to provide researchers with a strong intuition of each of the data processing
steps to help drive their future research and analysis.

This repository serves as a companion site with extended explanations, 
code, and reference material for attendees to revisit after 
the talk. A recording of the full presentation will be made available after the workshop.

## Tools & Methods Covered

- Introduction to the microbiome
- Brief overview of different types of microbiome data
- 16S rRNA sequencing overview
- Quality control and preprocessing
- Taxonomic classification
- Diversity analysis (alpha & beta)
- Differential abundance testing
